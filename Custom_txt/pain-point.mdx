# Pain-points

## 1. AI Chatbots (Aichatbot)

### Practical pain points:

#### Customers emailing or calling repeatedly for the same questions (order status, password reset, FAQs).

#### Support team overwhelmed, taking hours to answer simple queries.

#### Small team cannot provide 24/7 support → late-night or weekend inquiries ignored.

#### Mistakes in answers when human agents are tired or under pressure.

#### Employees wasting time asking HR or IT basic questions repeatedly.

#### Difficult to scale support without hiring more staff.



---

## 2. Custom ML (customml)

### Practical pain points:

#### Business has lots of data but no one can turn it into actionable predictions.

#### Sales/marketing teams can’t forecast demand, leads, or customer churn accurately.

#### Personalized product or content recommendations are missing → customers disengage.

#### ML models built by consultants never deployed; they “sit in notebooks.”

#### Current analytics slow or inaccurate; decision-making delayed.

#### Unable to automate repetitive tasks like data tagging or categorization.



---

## 3. DevOps

### Practical pain points:

#### Each new feature release takes weeks, causing delays in product launches.

#### Deployments often break live apps → downtime for customers.

#### Developers spend too much time fixing infrastructure issues instead of building new features.

#### Lack of automated testing → bugs slip through to production.

#### No alerts or monitoring; issues are discovered only when reported by users.

#### Collaboration issues between teams; release processes inconsistent.



---

## 4. Cloud Consulting

### Practical pain points:

#### Cloud bills spike unexpectedly → CFO complains monthly.

#### Servers/resources over-provisioned → paying for unused capacity.

#### Migration from on-premises systems to cloud is confusing and risky.

#### Security misconfigurations put sensitive data at risk.

#### Multiple cloud accounts, tools, and services are messy and hard to manage.

#### Poor scaling → website or app crashes during high traffic.

#### Team doesn’t know which cloud services are cost-effective for their workloads.



---

## 5. Software Development (Developer Services)

### Practical pain points:

#### Off-the-shelf software doesn’t support the team’s exact workflow.

#### Legacy apps are slow, buggy, or incompatible with modern devices.

#### Internal dev team is too small or inexperienced to build needed features.

#### Different tools don’t communicate → manual data entry and errors.

#### Projects take too long because requirements are unclear or constantly changing.

#### Employees avoid using poorly designed apps → low adoption.

#### No proper documentation → hard to maintain or scale apps in future.
