
# 🧩 Service Sub-Page Playbook (Step-by-Step)

---

## 1. **Hero Section (Above the Fold)**

**Framework:** AIDA (Attention → Interest → Desire → Action)

✅ What to include:

* **Headline** → Clear, outcome-driven.

  * *“Smarter AI Chatbots That Reduce Costs & Delight Customers 24/7.”*
* **Sub-headline** → Reinforce value in simple terms.

  * *“Automate customer support, capture leads, and scale conversations — without extra staff.”*
* **CTA** → 1 primary (Book Demo / Request Quote).
* **Visual** → Show the service in action (screenshot, mockup, illustration with people).

⚠️ Avoid:

* Buzzwords (“next-gen,” “revolutionary”).
* Talking about YOU first. Start with **customer benefit**.

---

## 2. **Logo Cloud / Trust Signals**

**Framework:** Authority-Building

✅ What to include:

* Logos of clients, partners, or certifications (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud).
* If no big logos yet → industry badges or “As featured in…” PR mentions.

⚠️ Avoid:

* Random logos with no context. Add a small caption like *“Trusted by businesses across industries.”*

---

## 3. **Problem → Solution Section**

**Framework:** PAS (Problem → Agitate → Solution)

✅ What to include:

* 2–3 pain points your audience feels.

  * *“Slow, repetitive customer queries drain your support team.”*
* Agitate briefly (make it feel urgent).
* Position your service as the fix.

**Visuals:**

* Use icons for each pain point → beside short text.

⚠️ Avoid:

* Overloading with technical jargon here. Keep it *human*.

---

## 4. **How It Works (Process Steps)**

**Framework:** SLAP (Stop → Look → Act → Purchase)

✅ What to include:

* 3–5 simple steps of your process.

  * *Consult → Design → Deploy → Optimize.*
* Each step = 1 icon + 1 sentence.

⚠️ Avoid:

* Paragraphs. This section must be *scannable*.

---

## 5. **Who This Is For (Persona Grid)**

**Framework:** StoryBrand (SB7 → Clarify audience’s role in the story)

✅ What to include:

* Grid of 3–4 personas/industries.

  * E.g., *E-commerce → Reduce cart abandonment.*
  * *SaaS → 24/7 customer support.*
  * *Enterprises → Automate internal queries.*
* Each persona = 1 icon + short benefit.

⚠️ Avoid:

* Writing too generic like *“for everyone.”* Get specific.

---

## 6. **Features → Benefits Section**

**Framework:** FAB (Features → Advantages → Benefits)

✅ What to include:

* 4–6 features, each tied to a benefit.

  * *Multi-channel → Talk to customers on web, WhatsApp, Messenger.*
  * *AI learning → Gets smarter over time.*
  * *Analytics → Track ROI in real time.*
* Layout: **Icon + Feature Title + 1 Benefit Line.**

⚠️ Avoid:

* Listing features without why they matter. (Always ask: “So what?”).

---

## 7. **Case Studies / Proof Section**

**Framework:** BAB (Before → After → Bridge)

✅ What to include:

* 2–3 case study cards (image, headline result, short summary).

  * *“Reduced support tickets by 45% with AI chatbot.”*
* CTA link → “See full case study.”

⚠️ Avoid:

* Wall of text. Use **results-first headlines.**

---

## 8. **Key Metrics / KPI Section**

**Framework:** Authority + Social Proof

✅ What to include:

* 3–4 big proof numbers (large font).

  * *“30% fewer support tickets.”*
  * *“<1s avg. response time.”*
  * *“20% more leads captured.”*
* Pair each metric with a small icon/visual.

⚠️ Avoid:

* Vanity metrics that don’t connect to customer value.

---

## 9. **FAQ Section**

**Framework:** Objection Handling

✅ What to include:
5–7 common questions like:

* How long does setup take?
* Can it integrate with my systems?
* Is it secure / GDPR compliant?
* Do I need technical skills?
* How do you price this service?

**Visuals:** Accordion dropdowns for scannability.

⚠️ Avoid:

* Overly technical answers. Keep FAQs simple + action-oriented.

---

## 10. **Final CTA (Conversion Push)**

**Framework:** The Four Ps (Picture → Promise → Prove → Push)

✅ What to include:

* Short headline: *“Ready to Automate Your Customer Experience?”*
* Subtext: Reinforce promise.
* CTA button: *“Book a Free Demo”* or *“Get Started Today.”*
* Visual: Customer + chatbot interaction, or smiling team member.

⚠️ Avoid:

* Ending with “Thanks for visiting.” Must close strong with *one action*.

---

# 🎨 Visual & Emotional Guidelines

* **Visual Storytelling > Text Blocks** → Icons, stats, mockups.
* **Human Imagery** → Real people, not just abstract servers/clouds.
* **Scannability** → Headlines + 1-liners + whitespace.
* **Emotional Resonance** → Speak to *you* (customer), not “we.”
* **Frameworks** → Used invisibly to shape copy, not labeled on page.

---

# 🔑 Psychological Flow Recap

1. *What is this?* → Hero
2. *Is it for me?* → Problem-Solution + Persona Grid
3. *How does it work?* → Process Steps
4. *What do I get?* → Features → Benefits
5. *Can I trust you?* → Case Studies + Metrics + FAQ
6. *What’s next?* → Final CTA
