1. Audience Research

Your likely target audience:

Demographics: Tech-savvy founders, CTOs, product managers, mid-size business owners, SaaS companies, startups, and sometimes non-technical entrepreneurs.

Psychographics: Efficiency-driven, growth-minded, cost-conscious, curious about AI/automation but often overwhelmed by complexity.

Pain points:

Customer support takes too much time/money.

Teams waste hours on repetitive tasks.

DevOps pipelines break or cost too much.

Data is underused because no ML expertise in-house.

Don’t know how to connect tools (Slack, CRM, databases).


Desires:

Save time with automation.

Reduce costs of hiring/ops.

Impress users with AI-powered features.

Scale infrastructure with confidence.


Objections:

“Too expensive.”

“Too complex for my business.”

“Will this actually work in my case?”

“Security/compliance concerns.”




---

2. Market & Competitor Research

Direct competitors: Chatbot agencies, DevOps consultancies, workflow automation specialists, n8n agencies.

Indirect competitors: Zapier consultants, RPA vendors, generic software agencies.

Common hooks competitors use:

“Save time & money with automation.”

“We make AI simple.”

“We streamline your DevOps.”


Gap you can fill: Many competitors sell only one service (e.g., just chatbots or just DevOps). You cover end-to-end automation + AI + DevOps → a stronger “integration partner” angle.



---

3. Product/Service Deep Dive

AI Chatbots → Feature: custom-trained bots. Benefit: reduce support costs, 24/7 availability, improved customer experience.

Custom ML → Feature: models tailored to business needs. Benefit: turn unused data into insights/revenue.

DevOps Consultancy → Feature: CI/CD pipelines, cloud scaling, monitoring. Benefit: faster deployments, less downtime, lower infra costs.

Workflow Automation → Feature: process automation (HR, finance, sales). Benefit: save hours daily, fewer manual errors.

n8n Automation → Feature: custom integrations across tools. Benefit: cheaper and more flexible than Zapier, fully self-hostable.


Unique Mechanism: You offer all-in-one automation expertise — AI + ML + DevOps + workflow automation. Few competitors combine these.

4. Voice of Customer (VoC) Research

Examples of what they might say:

“I’m drowning in repetitive tasks.”

“I’ve heard of automation but don’t know where to start.”

“I need a chatbot that actually understands my business, not a generic one.”

“Our DevOps costs are spiraling — we need help optimizing.”

“Zapier gets too expensive, we need something scalable.”


Use these phrases in your copy — it makes the visitor feel heard.


---

5. Conversion Research

Awareness stage visitors: Curious about AI/automation but don’t know solutions yet. They need education (“Here’s how AI saves you time & money”).

Desire stage visitors: Know about chatbots/automation but can’t choose. They need comparisons and proof.

Decision stage visitors: Already shopping. They need case studies, testimonials, pricing clarity.


Your homepage should have:

1. Simple explainer (for newbies).


2. Strong proof + results (for evaluators).


3. CTA to talk (for decision-ready buyers).

6. Brand Voice Research

Not too “tech-heavy” (many buyers are not technical).

Confident but not arrogant.

7. Emotional Research

Fears: Wasting money on failed tech, being left behind by AI wave, too complex to manage.

Dreams: Business runs on autopilot, team focuses on creativity/strategy instead of busywork, costs go down, innovation goes up.

Relief: “Finally, someone who can connect everything for me.”

Risk of not acting: Competitors move faster, higher costs, missed opportunities with AI.
