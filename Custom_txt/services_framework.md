# 🏗️ Service Page Copywriting Playbook

---

## **General Principles**

* Each service page = **a focused landing page** for one service.
* Visitors come here already curious — so the page should:

  1. Explain *what exactly the service is*.
  2. Show *why it matters to them*.
  3. Prove *you’re the best choice*.
  4. Make it *easy to act*.
* Use frameworks invisibly: **PAS, FAB, AIDA, StoryBrand**.
* **Write for scanners**: short blocks, icons, bold benefits, step visuals.
* **Emotional + logical flow**: Problem → Solution → Proof → CTA.

---

## **1. Hero Section 1**

**Purpose:** Capture attention, explain the service in 1 line, and push toward CTA.
**Frameworks:** AIDA (Attention + Interest), Outcome-Focused Copy.

* **What to Write:**

  * **Headline** → Service + core benefit.
    *“AI Chatbots That Handle Support So Your Team Doesn’t Have To.”*
  * **Subheadline** → Clarify who it’s for and why it matters.
    *“Designed for businesses tired of repetitive support tasks — faster, smarter, customer-friendly.”*
  * **CTA** → “Book a Free Demo” or “Get Started.”

* **Visuals:**

  * Hero illustration (chatbot mockup, workflow diagram).
  * 2–3 mini benefit callouts with icons.

---

## **2. Logo Cloud (Trust Builder)**

**Purpose:** Instant credibility for the specific service.
**Frameworks:** Authority-Building Copy.

* **What to Write:**

  * Logos of clients who used this service (or certifications).
  * If none: generic credibility line → *“Trusted by 50+ businesses across finance, healthcare, and retail.”*

* **Visuals:**

  * Row of 5–7 logos, greyscale.

---

## **3. How It Works (Process Steps)**

**Purpose:** Simplify complex service into 3–5 easy steps.
**Frameworks:** StoryBrand (Plan Step), SLAP (Stop → Look → Act → Purchase).

* **What to Write:**

  * Step 1: *“Book a consultation.”*
  * Step 2: *“We assess your needs and design a solution.”*
  * Step 3: *“Implementation with ongoing support.”*
  * Step 4 (Optional): *“Track results and optimize.”*

* **Dos:** Keep steps short, action-based.

* **Don’ts:** Don’t overexplain technical details here.

* **Visuals:**

  * Timeline graphic or numbered steps with icons.
  * Human illustration (someone guiding a process).

---

## **4. Who This Is For (Audience Grid)**

**Purpose:** Let people self-identify → “Yes, this is for me.”
**Frameworks:** Problem-Solution Copy, Persona Grid.

* **What to Write:**

  * Headline: *“Is This Service Right for You?”*
  * 3–4 personas with pain points + how service helps.

    * Example:

      * 🏢 **Small Businesses** → Save time and reduce repetitive tasks.
      * 🏦 **Enterprises** → Scale without hiring extra support staff.
      * 💻 **Startups** → Launch quickly without technical headaches.

* **Visuals:**

  * Grid layout (3–4 cards).
  * Each with persona icon + short benefit.

---

## **5. Features or Benefits Section**

**Purpose:** Show details of what’s included, but framed as **benefits**.
**Frameworks:** FAB (Features → Advantages → Benefits), Benefit-Stacking Copy.

* **What to Write:**

  * Headline: *“Why Our \[Service] Works for You.”*
  * 4–6 features → turn each into a benefit.

    * Example:

      * 🤖 **Smart AI Responses** → Faster, personalized replies.
      * 🔒 **Secure Cloud Hosting** → Keep data safe and compliant.
      * ⚡ **Quick Integration** → Get started in weeks, not months.
      * 📊 **Analytics Dashboard** → Track performance in real-time.

* **Visuals:**

  * Icon grid (2x2 or 3x2).
  * Each feature with headline + 1 line of benefit.

---

## **6. Case Studies (Proof in Action)**

**Purpose:** Show real-world outcomes of this service.
**Frameworks:** BAB (Before → After → Bridge).

* **What to Write:**

  * Headline: Customer success → *“Reduced support tickets by 40% in 2 months.”*
  * Small card: Problem → Solution → Result.
  * CTA: *“Read full story.”*

* **Visuals:**

  * 2–3 cards with logos, testimonial quotes, results metric.

---

## **7. Key Metrics Section (KPI Block)**

**Purpose:** Quantify results → turn promises into numbers.
**Frameworks:** Authority-Building Copy.

* **What to Write:**

  * 3–4 service-specific KPIs.

    * Example (AI Chatbot):

      * 60% fewer manual tickets.
      * 24/7 availability.
      * 95% customer satisfaction rate.

* **Visuals:**

  * Large bold numbers with small subtext.
  * Icons (clock, happy face, shield, chart).

---

## **8. Related Blog Posts (Education + SEO)**

**Purpose:** Build trust with thought leadership & keep users exploring.
**Frameworks:** Educational Copy, Full-Funnel Method.

* **What to Write:**

  * Headline: *“Learn More About \[Service].”*
  * 3–4 curated blog posts or guides related to the service.
  * Short headline + 1–2 sentence teaser.

* **Visuals:**

  * Blog card grid with thumbnails.

---

## **9. Hero Section 2 (Final CTA / Emotional Close)**

**Purpose:** End strong with outcome-focused CTA.
**Frameworks:** AIDA (Desire → Action), Outcome-Focused Copy.

* **What to Write:**

  * Headline: Aspirational outcome → *“Deliver Better Customer Experiences, Effortlessly.”*
  * Subheadline: 1–2 lines about why now is the time.
  * CTA: *“Book Your Free Consultation.”*

* **Visuals:**

  * Background image with humans (customer + success).
  * Large centered button.

---

# 🧠 Psychological Flow for Service Pages

1. **What is this service?** → Hero 1
2. **Can I trust you?** → Logo Cloud
3. **How does it work?** → Process Steps
4. **Is it right for me?** → Audience Grid
5. **What’s included / why is it valuable?** → Features/Benefits
6. **Will it work for others like me?** → Case Studies + Metrics
7. **Do you know your stuff?** → Related Blogs
8. **What should I do now?** → Final Hero (CTA)

---

# ✅ Dos & Don’ts for Service Pages

**Dos:**

* Speak directly to the customer’s pain points.
* Use icons, grids, and visuals for easy scanning.
* Include **proof (case studies, stats)** early and late in the page.
* Always end with a strong CTA.
* Keep tone human + empathetic.

**Don’ts:**

* Dump technical jargon.
* Overload with too many features (stick to top 4–6).
* Forget emotional resonance — services are still sold to people, not just companies.
